The product

Agent Gate

A single dashboard for site owners. See which agents arrive, decide who is allowed, set the price per page and watch the receipts come in.

Sign in with one passkey. No seed phrase, no browser extension.

  1. Identity

    Agents carry an onchain identity, so policy can tell a registered agent from an anonymous client.

  2. Pricing

    The owner sets a price for each site.

  3. Authorization

    Allow, price or block each agent by rule.

  4. Payment

    Agents pay in USDC on Monad.

  5. Delivery

    Content is served once payment clears.

  6. Receipt

    Every visit is receipted and billed.

  7. Analytics

    Who came, what they paid, which page earned it.
Approach

Built on a few firm ideas.

Owners first

The person who made the content decides who reads it and at what price.

Identity before access

A blocked request and a paid request should both be traceable to a known agent.

Settled, not promised

Payment happens onchain and every visit leaves a receipt the owner can check.
